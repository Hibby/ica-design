Stranraer Mixed Weekend

20–21 February 2027 · North West Castle, Stranraer

The ICA’s annual mixed weekend takes place on 20 and 21 February 2027 at North West Castle, Stranraer.

How to enter

Reply to the original ICA invitation to secure your team’s place. Please complete the form whether or not you’re coming.

The response form is included in the original event invitation.

Please respond by 17 December 2026.

Competition fees

Entry is £36 per team, payable to the ICA. Ice fees are additional for teams not staying at North West Castle. Hotel guests have their ice fees included. Use the original invitation for payment details.

Hotel package

  • Standard twin or double room, £250 per person.
  • Full board with single occupancy, £300.
  • Superior room upgrade, £10 per person per night.

The two-night full-board package includes dinner, bed and breakfast, two lunches and curling.
