About ICA

The Irish Curling Association brings Irish curlers together, supports competition and works to develop the sport on the island of Ireland.

Our work

Irish Curling works with World Curling and the Olympic Federation of Ireland. That includes taking part in the Irish Winter Sports Coordination Group and preparing teams for international competition.
