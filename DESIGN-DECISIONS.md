# Design decisions

- Lato is the primary typeface across the prototype. The package includes
  Regular, Bold and Black weights and the SIL Open Font License.

- The approved desktop and mobile concepts guide the compact banner, green
  navigation, four pathways and news layouts. Primary navigation uses direct
  links. Mobile puts the shortcuts in a two-column grid and stacks news as
  horizontal cards.
- The supplied ICA logo is used unchanged. The decorative banner is cropped
  from the generated desktop reference and is illustrative.
- News uses images extracted from the supplied Bonspiels and Fyfe Trophy,
  Belfast Trophy, and Euro B semi-final PDF exports. Article pages are
  prototype summaries of the original reports, with original dates,
  bylines and source links. Homepage news is newest first.
- Home and Events show the two upcoming events supplied for this review.
  Past events remain available on the Events page. The Mixed Weekend page
  refers readers to the original invitation for the response form and payment details. The prototype uses 17 December
  2026 as the example response deadline.
- The four supplied ICA PDF exports inform Developing Curling, History,
  Committee and Sponsorship. The committee is a dated snapshot, and
  the facilities examples are presented as historical context.
- The June 2026 President’s Report informs the Dublin ice proposal, member
  activity and recent international results. The 2025 AGM minutes corroborate
  the development priority and senior women’s bronze. The Dublin wording is
  tied to the June report. Confirm the planning and ice-access position before
  publication. Review the prototype copy for public use before publishing.
- Reconcile the name and committee presentation before publication. The 2026
  report records approval of the change from Irish Curling Association to
  Irish Curling. The 2025 minutes record election of a Vice President and
  trustees, whereas the supplied public committee page lists a vacant
  President-Elect role and a different committee structure. Confirm the name,
  officer list and charity registration status before editing the public
  header and governance pages. The 2025 vote authorised an application.
- The two AGM sources use different 2024–25 membership baselines. Resolve
  this before publishing a growth figure.
- Members Area is a local placeholder. Its content and access workflow
  remain open questions. The Roll of Honour is a proposed page awaiting
  verified historical records.
- The prototype is for reviewing the design and navigation. WordPress theme
  and publishing decisions, content preservation and URL continuity belong
  to the subsequent implementation assessment.

## Outbound link audit for v0.5

The prototype has no external navigation. These were the live ICA links
in v0.4 and how each is handled now.

| Previous destination | Treatment in the demo |
| --- | --- |
| ICA contact page | Local contact form with a disabled send button |
| Full ICA history | Full text from the four-page export on the local History page |
| Full ICA events calendar | Local upcoming and past event listings |
| Original ICA sponsorship page | Local sponsorship information |
| ICA Members Area | Local placeholder with a link to public membership information |
| Three original ICA reports | Local article pages link back to the demo’s News page |

The Stranraer Mixed Weekend also previously linked to an external Microsoft
response form. The demo now refers readers to the original invitation.
